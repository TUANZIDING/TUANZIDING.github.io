01为什么需要 CADSWhy CADS

CT 全身分割能把影像变成结构化的解剖信息，用于放疗计划、异常检测与分诊、转移灶定位和身体成分分析。Whole-body CT segmentation turns images into structured anatomy for radiotherapy planning, abnormality detection and triage, metastasis localisation and body-composition analysis.[1],[14],[15]

论文事实FACT · paper标注稀缺Labels are scarce

本研究纳入的 40 个公开 CT 来源中，只有 21% 的扫描带解剖标注，而且大多只标了一两个结构。Across the 40 public CT sources in this study, only 21% of scans had anatomical annotations, mostly of one or two structures.

论文事实FACT · paper模型碎片化Fragmented models

多数 AI 方法只针对单一器官，标签定义不一致，训练与评估方案各不相同。Most AI methods are organ-specific, with inconsistent label definitions and heterogeneous training and evaluation protocols.

论文事实FACT · paper现有资源的缺口Gaps in existing resources

解剖覆盖有限、数据多样性不足、复杂结构存在系统性标注错误、依赖人工流程、外部验证不足、模型或数据不开放、缺少易用工具。Limited anatomical coverage, insufficient diversity, systematic label errors in complex structures, human-dependent workflows, limited external validation, restricted availability and a lack of accessible tools.

教学解读TEACHING一句话理解：CADS 的贡献不是新的网络结构，而是“数据为中心”——把碎片化的公开数据整理成统一、完整、可复用的标注，再用成熟的 nnU-Net 训练。论文原话：“The contribution is not a new network architecture”。In one line: CADS contributes a data-centric resource, not a new network. The paper says so directly: "The contribution is not a new network architecture" — it curates fragmented public data into consistent, complete labels and trains an established nnU-Net on them.

02CADS-dataset：22,022 个 CTCADS-dataset: 22,022 CT volumes

把公开档案、挑战赛数据和两份新医院数据整合到同一个解剖标签空间。Public archives, challenge datasets and two new hospital cohorts merged into one anatomical label space.

论文事实FACT · paper规模与来源Scale and sources[1]

  • 22,022 个 CT，来自 40 个来源、16 个国家、100 多个影像中心，采集年份 2007–2024。22,022 CT volumes from 40 sources, 16 countries and more than 100 centres, acquired 2007–2024.
  • 其中只有 4,695 个（21%）带部分标注；约 79% 完全没有标注。Only 4,695 (21%) carry partial annotations; about 79% have none.
  • 与 TotalSegmentator 数据集相比：扫描数约 18 倍，独立目标多 60%。Versus the TotalSegmentator dataset: about 18× as many scans and 60% more distinct targets.[3]
  • 大宗来源：NLST 7,172、CT-RATE 3,134、TotalSegmentator 1,203（104 个结构的标注）、AbdomenCT-1K 1,062。Largest sources: NLST 7,172, CT-RATE 3,134, TotalSegmentator 1,203 (annotated for 104 structures), AbdomenCT-1K 1,062.
  • 新贡献：484 个头部 CT（土耳其，Istanbul Medipol University 伦理批准）与 586 个三期增强腹部 CT（德国，TUM 伦理批准）。New contributions: 484 head CTs (Türkiye; Istanbul Medipol University ethics approval) and 586 triphasic contrast-enhanced abdominal CTs (Germany; TUM ethics approval).
  • 涵盖健康人与多种疾病：肿瘤（癌、淋巴瘤、转移）、COVID-19、创伤和罕见病；增强与平扫；头颈、胸、腹、盆、脊柱及全身视野。Healthy and pathological cases — cancers, lymphoma, metastases, COVID-19, trauma, rare disorders; contrast and non-contrast; head–neck, chest, abdomen, pelvis, spine and whole-body fields of view.

论文事实FACT · paper统一预处理Harmonised preprocessing[1]

  • 全部重定向到 RAS 坐标，并重采样到 1.5 mm 各向同性。All scans reoriented to RAS and resampled to 1.5 mm isotropic.
  • 简化仿射矩阵：去除旋转与平移，保留缩放与剪切，便于形状质控和跨扫描比较。Affines simplified by removing rotation and translation (keeping scale and shear) to support shape-based QC and cross-scan comparison.
  • 新头部 CT 的面部区域用高斯滤波模糊（1.5 mm 下 σ = 5 体素）以降低可识别性。Faces in the new head CTs blurred with a Gaussian filter (σ = 5 voxels at 1.5 mm) to reduce identifiability.
  • 标签空间以 SNOMED CT 为参照：每个目标给出 SNOMED 编码、包含/重叠关系与标注规范（补充表 1）。Label space referenced to SNOMED CT: every target has a SNOMED code, containment/overlap relations and a contouring convention (Suppl. Table 1).

教学解读TEACHING教学提示：1.5 mm 各向同性重采样意味着耳蜗（中位仅 33 体素）这类小结构天然受部分容积效应限制——这也解释了后面小结构分割较难。Teaching tip: at 1.5 mm isotropic, tiny targets such as the cochlea (median 33 voxels) are inherently limited by partial-volume effects — which foreshadows why small structures are harder later.

03从稀疏标注到 167 类全身标签：四步流程From sparse labels to 167-class whole-body labels: the four-step pipeline

两轮迭代：先用全标注数据训练模型生成伪标签，再用筛选后的标签训练最终模型。Two iterations: models trained on fully annotated data generate pseudo-labels; curated labels then train the final model.

全身伪标注Whole-body pseudo-labelling

论文事实FACT · paper把 167 个目标分成 9 个解剖组，每组训练一个专用 nnU-Net，初始训练数据来自 5 个来源：TotalSegmentator（104 类，构成 9 组中的 5 组）、VISCERAL 上新勾画的 10 个放疗危及器官、HaN-Seg 头颈结构、经 ANTs 配准 SimNIBS 图谱得到的脑结构、SAROS 体腔/组织（2D 稀疏标注层间插值成 3D）。随后对 22,022 个 CT 全部生成伪标签。Split the 167 targets into 9 groups and train a specialised nnU-Net per group from 5 sources: TotalSegmentator (104 classes, forming 5 of the 9 groups), 10 newly contoured radiotherapy OARs on VISCERAL, HaN-Seg head–neck structures, brain labels from the SimNIBS atlas via ANTs registration, and SAROS cavities/tissues (sparse 2D slices interpolated to 3D). Then pseudo-label all 22,022 CTs.

形状质控Shape-based quality control

论文事实FACT · paper用神经隐式函数的 auto-decoder 只在真实标注上学习“正常形状”；对每个伪标签求最能重建它的隐编码，以伪标签与重建之间的 90 百分位 Hausdorff 距离作为异常分数。总分最差的 10% 图像不进入 Shape 版本的训练集。An auto-decoder based on neural implicit functions learns plausible shapes from ground truth only; each pseudo-label is reconstructed by optimising a latent code, and the 90th-percentile Hausdorff distance between label and reconstruction is the outlier score. The worst-scoring 10% of images are withheld from the Shape-flavour training set.[7]

逐结构选标签Structure-wise label selection

论文事实FACT · paper训练三种“口味”模型：GT（只用原始真标注）、Pseudo（全部伪标签）、Shape（质控后伪标签）。每个结构在验证集上按 Dice 排名（优先用来源不匹配的 OOD 验证），p < 0.05 无差异时用 HD95 决胜。最终标签：原始真标注优先，其余用选中口味的预测，并按可靠性逐步合并。Train three flavours: GT (original labels only), Pseudo (all pseudo-labels) and Shape (QC-filtered pseudo-labels). For each structure, rank by Dice on validation (out-of-distribution when available); ties at p < 0.05 broken by HD95. Final labels: original ground truth first, otherwise the selected flavour, merged in order of reliability.

训练最终模型Train the final model

论文事实FACT · papernnU-Net v2.5.1 的残差编码器 U-Net（ResEnc-L），按类出现概率的倒数做类均衡采样——例如 C1–C5 椎骨只出现在 905–3,255 个 CT 中，而肝、脾、主动脉都超过 21,000 个。9 个分区模型共同覆盖 167 个目标。nnU-Net v2.5.1 residual-encoder U-Net (ResEnc-L) with class-balanced sampling weighted by inverse class-occurrence probability — e.g. C1–C5 each appear in only 905–3,255 CTs, while liver, spleen and aorta each exceed 21,000. Nine region models together cover all 167 targets.[5],[6]

教学解读TEACHING类比：Pseudo 像“大量实习生勾画”，Shape 像“先让主任按解剖常识剔除明显离谱的勾画”，GT 像“少量专家金标准”。CADS 不是只信一种，而是对每个结构挑在外部数据上表现最好的那一种。论文讨论部分指出，多模型平均主要降低伪标签中随机（方差）部分的误差；系统性错误需要像保留肋椎关节这样的定向解剖修正。Analogy: Pseudo is "many trainees contouring", Shape is "a senior removing anatomically absurd contours first", GT is "a small expert gold standard". CADS picks, per structure, whichever generalises best. The Discussion notes that averaging across models mainly reduces the random (variance) part of pseudo-label error; systematic errors need targeted anatomical fixes such as preserving costovertebral joints.

12局限与注意事项Limitations and cautions

论文事实FACT · paper论文自述的局限The paper's own limitations

  • 伪标签：形状质控会把低质量伪标签挡在 Shape 版本之外，但也可能滤掉有临床价值的病理或变异解剖；大规模下无法逐例人工修正。Pseudo-labels: shape QC withholds low-quality pseudo-labels from the Shape flavour, but may also filter clinically important pathological or atypical anatomy; manual correction is not feasible at this scale.
  • 临床评估：只有一位、未设盲的放射肿瘤医师做定性审阅，目的是找优缺点而非证明临床效用；需要多读者、盲法和临床终点。Clinical evaluation: a single non-blinded radiation oncologist reviewed to find strengths and weaknesses, not to prove clinical utility; multiple readers, blinding and clinical endpoints are needed.
  • 覆盖范围：头到膝，不含手足；中枢神经、肌骨和血管系统的精细结构仍缺。Coverage: head to knees, without hands and feet; fine-grained CNS, musculoskeletal and vascular structures are still missing.
  • 标签定义：脑掩膜包含颅内全部中枢神经成分（某些用途过宽）；气管只含气腔不含软骨；视交叉偏小；直肠短于解剖范围；面部去标识化限制了面部分析。Label definitions: the brain mask includes all intracranial CNS components (too broad for some uses); trachea covers the lumen, not cartilage; optic chiasm under-segmented; rectum shorter than its anatomical extent; facial anonymisation limits facial analysis.
  • 提升来源：数据规模、覆盖、多样性和标签整理同时变化，不能只归因于数据量。Source of gains: scale, coverage, diversity and curation all changed, so gains cannot be attributed to size alone.

教学解读TEACHING教学与使用提醒Teaching and usage reminders

  • 分割 ≠ 诊断：CADS 不识别骨折、出血、肿瘤等病灶，正常解剖标签在病变区域可能失真。Segmentation ≠ diagnosis: CADS does not detect fractures, haemorrhage or tumours, and normal-anatomy labels may distort around lesions.
  • 许可分层：代码 Apache-2.0；权重分 reference（定制）、research（CC BY-NC-SA 4.0）、open（CC BY-SA 4.0）；数据按 40 个来源各自的许可。用于商业或临床前请逐项核对。Layered licences: code Apache-2.0; weights reference (customised), research (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), open (CC BY-SA 4.0); data follow each of the 40 sources' licences. Check each before commercial or clinical use.
  • 本页 CPU 推理只有 1 例，且偏离官方配置（块大小、裁剪、无后处理），不能用来评价性能。The CPU inference here is one case with deviations from the official setup (patch size, crop, no post-processing); it cannot be used to judge performance.
  • 论文目前是 Article in Press（加速发表版），最终版本可能还有编辑改动；本页数字以 2026-11-13 获取的版本为准。The paper is an Article in Press; the final Version of Record may still change. Numbers here follow the version retrieved on 2026-11-13.
